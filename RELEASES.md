# Releases

Highlights for each version of the EVAPPS4U privacy policy. The detailed log
lives in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).

## 1.1.0 — Unreleased

The policy now covers apps that ask for ad consent and sell in-app purchases.
This comes first with Lucky Numbers and applies to every EVAPPS4U app that does
either.

- **Consent choices.** It explains Google's consent form, which appears where
  privacy law requires it, and how to change your choice later from the app.
- **In-app purchases.** Google Play handles payment, and we never see your
  card details.
- **Working language links.** Switching language no longer leads to a missing
  page.

## 1.0.0 — 20 February 2026

First version: one policy for all EVAPPS4U apps, in 12 languages.
