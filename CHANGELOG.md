# Changelog

All notable changes to the EVAPPS4U privacy policy site
(https://privacy.edgestoneventures.com/) are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).
A MINOR version is a change to what the policy says. A PATCH is a fix to the
site that leaves the policy text unchanged.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Google Play Billing and Google User Messaging Platform under Third-Party
  Services, in all 12 languages.
- "Your Consent Choices" and "In-App Purchases" sections, and two matching
  Summary bullets, in all 12 languages.

### Changed

- "Last Updated" is now November 24, 2026 on every page.

### Fixed

- Language links pointed at `/privacy-policy/<lang>/`, which 404s on the
  custom domain. They now point at `/<lang>/`.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-02-20

### Added

- EVAPPS4U privacy policy in 12 languages, served from the custom domain
  privacy.edgestoneventures.com.
