Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Edgestone Ventures, operating under the developer name EVAPPS4U ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how data is handled. This privacy policy explains how our mobile applications ("Apps") handle information.

This policy applies to all Apps published by EVAPPS4U on the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, or any other platform.

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Directly Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our own servers. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Login credentials or account information
  • Any data transmitted to servers owned or operated by us

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Our Apps use third-party advertising services, specifically Google AdMob, to display ads. These services may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers: Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address: Used to determine approximate location for serving relevant ads
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, screen size, and language settings
  • Ad interaction data: Information about ads viewed, clicked, or interacted with
  • App usage data: General usage patterns and diagnostic information

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Our Apps may store preferences and settings locally on your device only, such as:

  • App Settings: Your customization preferences and configuration choices
  • User Preferences: Display settings, theme choices, and other personalization options

This data is stored using your device's built-in storage mechanisms and never leaves your device. It is not accessible to us or any third parties.

How We Use Information

We do not directly collect or use your personal data. The locally stored preferences are used solely to:

  • Remember your customization choices
  • Provide a personalized user experience within the App

Data collected by Google AdMob is used by Google to serve advertisements. We do not have access to the personal data collected by Google through their advertising SDK.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information. However, our Apps use the Google AdMob SDK, which shares data with Google for the purpose of serving advertisements. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Data Security

Your local preferences are stored securely on your device using built-in secure storage mechanisms. For data collected by third-party advertising services, please refer to their respective privacy policies for information about their security practices.

Third-Party Services

Our Apps integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements within our Apps. Google AdMob may collect device and usage data as described above. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use their services.

Ad Personalization

Google AdMob may use collected data to show personalized advertisements. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising > Turn off Personalized Ads

Permissions

Our Apps may request device permissions necessary for their core functionality. These permissions are used solely for the App's intended purpose. Specific permissions required by each App are listed on its store listing page.

Additionally, an internet connection is required to load and display advertisements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Your Rights

Since we do not directly collect any personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, modify, or delete. Your local preferences can be cleared by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App. For data collected by Google, you can manage your data and privacy settings through your Google Account.

Data Retention

We do not retain any data on our servers because we do not collect any data directly. Local preferences remain on your device until you:

  • Clear the App's data
  • Uninstall the App
  • Reset preferences within the App (if available)

For data retained by Google through AdMob, please refer to Google's data retention policies.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our Apps' privacy practices, please contact us at:

Email: apps@edgestoneventures.com

Summary

  • We do not directly collect any personal data
  • All app preferences are stored locally on your device
  • Our Apps use Google AdMob for advertising, which collects device and usage data
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • We do not sell or trade your information

Your privacy is important to us, and all EVAPPS4U applications are built with transparency in mind.
